# Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the Google Cloud OAuth application used by Andrea Longo to access his own Google Drive through rclone, an open source file transfer program.

- **Who uses it:** only Andrea Longo, on his own computers. The application is not offered to the public.
- **Data accessed:** files and folders in Andrea Longo's own Google Drive, to upload and download his work files.
- **Where data goes:** the access token and the transferred files stay on Andrea Longo's computers and in his Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other server or third party.
- **No other use:** no data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
- **Revoking access:** access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account, under Security, third-party apps with account access.
- The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
